MEET RANJU / THE MAKER

Ghar ka swaad.
Haath se bana.

For nearly 50 years, Ranju has been making food for her family at home. She has always found joy in feeding people — turning everyday ingredients into something warm, familiar and worth sharing.

Born and raised in Bihar, she grew up with thekua as a beloved local snack: crisp, comforting and made for chai, journeys, festivals and family gatherings. Over the years, she tried different recipes, textures and levels of sweetness to create the kind of thekua each person would enjoy.

Her cooking is shaped by her Mithila roots and its love of ghee-rich food traditions. Today, she brings that home-kitchen care to Ranju's Kitchen: handmade, small-batch thekua prepared with patience and packed for you to enjoy at home.

“Make something with care, then make sure there is enough to share.”
